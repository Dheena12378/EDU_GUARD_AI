# 5-Minute Hackathon Demo Script — EDU CARD AI

**Theme:** AI-Assisted Academic Monitoring with Proactive Support & Ethical AI  
**Time Limit:** 5 Minutes  
**Target Audience:** Hackathon Judges, Academic Leaders, Learning Technologists  

---

## ⏱️ Minute 0:00 – 1:00 | The Hook & The Problem
> *"Good morning judges. In universities worldwide, academic interventions usually happen when it is already too late — after midterms fail, after deadlines pass, and after students drop out.*
>
> *Most existing predictive tools make things worse: they act as 'student failure predictors', stamping stigmatizing labels on vulnerable learners.*
>
> *Today, we introduce **EDU CARD AI** — an AI system that detects subtle shifts in engagement **weeks before** grades drop, explains why in plain language, and assists mentors in providing optional, voluntary support.*
>
> *Our core philosophy is simple: **DETECT ➔ EXPLAIN ➔ ALERT ➔ SUPPORT ➔ HUMAN REVIEW**.*
> *The AI assists; it never judges, punishes, or ranks students."*

---

1. **Open the Login Page:**

   - *"Every screen displays our mandatory AI assistance disclaimer."*

---

## ⏱️ Minute 2:30 – 3:45 | Explainability & Playbook Action
1. **Plain-Language Reasoning (XAI):**
   - *"Look at the 'Why did this indicator appear?' panel. Instead of opaque neural network weights, our SHAP synthesis produces plain-language observations: 'LMS Logins dropped 42% vs personal baseline'."*
   - *"Our system strictly enforces a Banned Words dictionary — zero stigmatizing terms like 'failing' or 'at-risk' can ever be outputted."*
2. **Interactive Counterfactual Simulator:**
   - *"What can Aarav do? We provide counterfactuals. If he logs in 3+ days this week and turns in his lab, the indicator immediately shifts back to Low (0.24). This empowers agency instead of helplessness."*
3. **Support Playbook & Human Review:**
   - *"Under Support Playbook, the system suggests tailored options from our institutional playbook. Let's launch 'Assignment Support & Flexible Extension'."*
   - Click **Initiate Support Check-in**, add a note: *"Offered 3-day extension on Lab 3"*, and click **Confirm**.
   - *"The action is logged in an immutable audit trail. The human mentor remains in complete control."*

---

## ⏱️ Minute 3:45 – 4:30 | Privacy & Algorithmic Fairness
1. **Switch to Student View:**
   - Use the Topbar Role Switcher to switch to **Student (Aarav Sharma)**.
   - *"When Aarav logs in, notice what he sees: no probability scores, no peer comparisons, and no labels that cause anxiety. Only supportive check-in suggestions and his personal study rhythm."*
2. **Algorithmic Fairness (Switch to Admin / Analytics):**
   - Switch to **Admin** and navigate to **Trends & Fairness**.
   - *"We implemented architectural Demographic Isolation: sensitive categories and genders are stored in a separate table and never used in model features.*
   - *Our Disparate Impact audit proves an exact parity ratio between 0.95 and 1.05 across all social categories and genders — mathematically zero demographic bias."*
